# Changelog

All notable changes to Pump Operator Hydraulics are listed here, newest first.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-29

### Changed
- New app icon: the Pump Operator Hydraulics Maltese cross badge

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Basement floors −1 to −5 on the floor setting. Each floor below street level takes 5 psi off.

### Changed
- Nozzle elevation wheel moves in 5 ft steps instead of 1 ft. Saved values snap to the nearest 5 ft.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

First release.

### Added
- Pump pressure calculator for single line, wyed lines, master stream (monitor or deck gun) and standpipe operations
- Scroll wheels for nozzle, hose, tip size, flow, length, nozzle pressure and elevation (feet or floors)
- Pump discharge pressure pinned at the top of the screen, with an NP / FL / AL / EP breakdown beside it
- Step-by-step math on a swipe-over screen
- Gating guidance for wyed lines
- Warning when pump pressure goes over 250 psi
- Quick calcs: friction loss, preconnect pump chart, smooth bore tip flow and elevation pressure
- Hydrant percentage method for estimating available water
- Editable friction loss coefficients and rules, saved on the device
- Light and dark themes
- Installs on Android and iPhone and works offline
