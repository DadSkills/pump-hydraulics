    How the numbers are built

    • Pump discharge pressure = NP + TPL, where total pressure loss (TPL) = friction loss + appliance loss + elevation pressure.
    • Friction loss: FL = C × Q² × L. Q is flow in hundreds of gpm and L is length in hundreds of feet.
    • Smooth bore flow: GPM = 29.7 × d² × √NP, rounded to the whole gallon before friction loss is figured.
    • Nozzle pressure: fog 100 psi (low-pressure fog 75 or 50), smooth bore handline 50, smooth bore master stream 80.
    • Appliances: 0 psi under 350 gpm, 10 psi at 350 gpm or more. Master stream devices 25 psi at any flow. Standpipe systems 25 psi.
    • Elevation: 0.5 psi per foot, or 5 psi per floor above the first. Nozzles below the pump give a negative value.
    • Wyed lines: pump to the line that needs the most pressure and gate the others down at the wye.
    • Siamesed lines of equal length: use the siamese coefficient with the total flow.

    Training and preplanning aid. Coefficients and rules are the standard values taught in driver/operator courses. Your department's pump charts, SOPs and the hose and pump ratings take precedence.
